Dominoes Offline: Classic Game — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Developer and publisher: KUPTSOV KIRILL (trading as Qira Studio), Spain.

Dominoes Offline: Classic Game ("the app") is a dominoes game played against computer opponents. The game itself works offline, has no accounts and no analytics. The Android version shows ads from Google AdMob, which is described below. The iOS app and the browser version contain no ads and collect no data.

Data we store on your device

Game settings (number of players, game type, rules, difficulty, language, sound), your progress (tournaments, coins, achievements) and your win statistics are stored only locally on your device and are never sent to us.

Ads (Android)

The Android version shows full-screen ads between matches, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. We do not receive this data and do not use it for any other purpose. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you are asked for consent before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time under "Ad privacy choices" in the app menu. You can also reset or disable the advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses data in ads.

Permissions

The Android version uses internet access (to load ads) and the advertising ID permission. The game does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address.
